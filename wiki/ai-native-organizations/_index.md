# AI-Native Organizations

AI-native organizations are companies whose data, policies, permissions, workflows, and measurement loops are redesigned so agents can execute structured work while humans supervise judgment and exceptions.

## Articles
- [[how-to-become-ai-native|How to Become AI-Native]] — AI-native companies make themselves legible to agents through clean data, explicit workflow rules, access boundaries, and measurable business outcomes.
- [[own-your-intelligence|Own Your Intelligence]] — Durable AI advantage comes from owning context, governance, feedback loops, and agent operating systems around generic models.
- [[ai-native-enterprise-operating-environment|AI-Native Enterprise Operating Environment]] — Enterprise readiness for agents through explicit workflows, credential scope, policy surfaces, data contracts, and approval lanes.

# Own Your Intelligence

## Summary

The LangChain “Own Your Intelligence” source argues that durable AI advantage comes from owning the agent systems around generic models: context, governance, feedback loops, workflows, evaluation, and institutional learning. The claim fits the broader AI-native organization pattern: the model is not the moat; the operating system around the model is.

## Key Takeaways

- Generic models are increasingly available to everyone, so advantage shifts toward proprietary context, workflow integration, governance, and feedback loops.
- Owning intelligence means owning how agents are built, measured, improved, deployed, and constrained inside the organization.
- Governance is part of product quality, not a separate compliance afterthought, because agent behavior changes with context and feedback.
- Feedback loops convert production traces, user behavior, eval failures, and human review into compounding institutional knowledge.
- AI-native organizations need both reusable platforms and local domain judgment; outsourcing the whole intelligence layer weakens learning.
- The article reinforces the wiki’s existing AI-native theme: make the organization legible to agents, then keep the improvement loop internal.

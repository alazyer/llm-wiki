# SEO and Social Agent Loops

## Summary

The fetched AI Builder Club SEO and social-loop articles show loop engineering applied to public growth channels. The point is not that agents should blindly post more content; it is that search and social workflows can be decomposed into scouts, generators, monitors, scorecards, and human gates with explicit no-op conditions and external feedback signals.

## Key Takeaways

- Growth loops need external signals, such as Search Console positions or public social response, so the loop is not grading its own homework.
- SEO loops should score young terms on daily position series and footprint, not trailing window averages that hide rank collapse.
- A missing or zero-impression day is not a first-place ranking; monitoring rules must distinguish absent data from good data.
- Social cold-launch loops should separate idea scouting, draft generation, review, posting, and feedback ingestion instead of letting one prompt own the entire cycle.
- Every content loop needs a quality valve that can ship nothing and record why; otherwise the loop manufactures weak articles or posts to satisfy cadence.
- Human merge/post approval remains a throughput constraint that the loop should report, not route around.
